Every Mother, Every Article

✓ Reviewed · merged 06 Nov 2026 Short review PR stecd/direito-de-mae#2

Short review: what matters here is which sources were used, what they corrected and what's still open, and that reads best as one scroll. It's stacked on PR 1, so merge that first. To comment, select any text and use the comment control in the artifact viewer.

What changed

The bot used to know one kind of mother: a domestic worker. Now it asks what kind of work she does and answers from the rules for that work:

CLT and domesticJob protection
INSS (self-employed, MEI, unemployed, rural)10 contributions
Federal servantFrom 9th month
Empresa Cidadã+60 days

Everything in laws.md was checked word for word against the official compiled texts. A weekly job now watches those laws and opens a GitHub issue when one changes.

Where the law text came from

planalto.gov.br blocked every request: the shell, the built-in browser, three fetch agents and your own Chrome. The text came instead from the Senado's and the Câmara's official compilations, which are as official as planalto. Every recent amending law was also read in its original published form, so each compiled article is cross-checked against the law that changed it. The binding text is still the DOU's.

The check changed three things in the old laws.md:

laws.md
-- CF art. 7º, parágrafo único (EC 72/2013): estendeu às domésticas ...+(cited as CF art. 7º, §1º: article 7º has no parágrafo único)-- Vale mesmo que a patroa não soubesse da gravidez.-- Se for dispensada nesse período: pode pedir reintegração ou indenização.+- Demissão por discriminação: ... voltar ao trabalho recebendo todo o período,+  ou receber em dobro (Lei 9.029 art. 4º).

The two deleted claims may well be true in court, but no article we read says them, and the bot may only say what it can cite.

It also turned up four laws from the last 16 months that the old file didn't know about: Zika (+60 days), hospital stays (leave runs 120 days from discharge), the INSS 30-day deadline, and the father's new leave from 01/01/2027 (10, then 15, then 20 days, the last stage depending on a fiscal target).

The weekly check

check_laws.py runs every Monday and looks at three things against a recorded baseline:

  • the Senado's index of each law, which lists every act that amended it;
  • a fingerprint of each watched article;
  • DOU publications from the last week that name these laws.

Only a new amending act or changed article text reaches mothers, as one added sentence: "a lei pode ter mudado". Layout changes and DOU mentions go to the GitHub issue only. Nothing edits laws.md automatically.

The review

The co-worker spot-checked the legal claims against the Senado text and stress-tested the checker. Round 1 requested changes and round 2 approved.

  • fixedThe INSS 30-day deadline had no article number and wasn't watched. It now cites Lei 8.213 art. 73-A, and Leis 15.415 and 15.371 are watched sources.
  • fixedA crash in the checker would have been filed as "a law changed". Crashes now fail the run, and only real changes open an issue.
  • fixedA website redesign would have put "the law may have changed" on every answer to every mother. Now only an amending act or changed article text triggers it.
  • fixedAn outage during the first baseline would have silently stopped the checker watching that law. Recording a baseline now refuses unless every source was read.
  • fixedThe father's pay was attributed to the INSS. A CLT employer pays and is reimbursed (Lei 8.213 arts. 73-D, 73-E).
  • confirmedThe co-worker fetched Lei 15.371 itself and confirmed the 10/15/20-day schedule and its fiscal condition.

Limits

  • The checker has never run against the live sites; only saved test pages. Its first Monday run on GitHub is the real test. If the Senado markup differs from what the parser expects, it reports "not found", and you'll see that in the issue.
  • The DOU search format is a guess, because in.gov.br dropped every connection during the build. If it's wrong, the DOU signal finds nothing, and the two Senado signals still work.
  • The warning only reaches mothers if the server pulls the repo. There's no production deployment yet, so nothing pulls today.
  • STF ADI 7.495 (equal 120 + 60 days for all mothers) is recorded in the Senado's index but couldn't be read on the STF site, so the bot doesn't rely on it. Servants who adopt, and state or municipal servants, go to HR, the union and the Defensoria.
  • Not run live against Claude: whether it reliably asks the kind of work before giving deadlines is untested.
