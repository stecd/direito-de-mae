Forget the Voice

Awaiting your pass · unmerged Short review PR stecd/direito-de-mae#5

Short review: one mechanism, so this page is one scroll. To comment, select any text and use the comment control in the artifact viewer.

What changed

ElevenLabs keeps the text of every voice it generates in our account's history. With voice replies on, a line like "Anotei: a patroa disse que vai te mandar embora" would sit there indefinitely, beyond the reach of our encryption and APAGAR. Now each personal reply is deleted from that history as soon as it has been generated.

Checked live against ElevenLabs

Before deletionThe 25-character test reply was in history: 1 item.
Afterforget deleted 1; history then held 0.

The welcome, consent and other fixed messages stay in the history. They hold nothing about her, they're already cached in public/voice/, and the free import script finds them there.

The one decision worth reading

The deletion sits in a finally and runs in the background. ElevenLabs records the text the moment it's requested, not when the audio comes back. So a reply must be deleted even if ffmpeg or the download fails afterwards, and the mother shouldn't wait for two extra API calls before hearing it.

The review

The co-worker was asked whether any personal reply could escape deletion. Round 1 requested changes. Round 2 approved.

  • blockingDeletion ran only after a successful generation. If ffmpeg crashed, the text stayed in history with no error. It now runs in a finally, with a test that crashes the generation.
  • fixedTwo extra API calls delayed every voice reply. Deletion now runs in the background.
  • fixedOnly the newest 20 items were searched. It now searches up to 3 pages of 20.

Limits

  • If deletion fails (permission revoked, ElevenLabs down), the text stays in their history. Only an error line in our log tells you.
  • A restart in the second after a reply kills the background deletion, without an error.
  • Her own voice notes go to ElevenLabs speech-to-text. Whether ElevenLabs keeps them isn't covered here. The bot's privacy facts already say the tech companies "podem guardar cópias por um tempo", and that stays true.
