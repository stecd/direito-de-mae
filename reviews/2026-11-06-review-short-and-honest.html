The one decision worth reading

The bot now classifies her by the job she had when she got pregnant or was dismissed, not by her situation today. Rights under the labour laws (CLT and LC 150, the domestic-worker law) attach to that job. A mother who was fired while pregnant is not "desempregada" for this purpose: that label is only for someone already without a job before the pregnancy. More than 2 days a week in one house makes her a domestic worker, with or without carteira, whatever she calls herself. When it's unclear whether a right applies, the bot says it may apply and sends her to the sindicato. It never stays silent.

The classification is stated back to her ("Entendi: você trabalha como doméstica… Certo?"), shown in MEUS DADOS, and passed to Claude on every message as "possibly wrong". So a correction like "na verdade são 3 dias" takes effect.

Smaller changes

  • The privacy facts the bot may state are written into its instructions. Tests tie them to the code, so the 90/365 days can't drift from store.py.
  • No instruction tells her to type an exact word any more.
  • Consent and MEUS DADOS no longer say "não guardo seu nome" (her name is in whatever she writes) or "senha" (it's a key).
  • A first message that is only a greeting ("oi") isn't answered a second time after consent. That saves a Claude call and, in voice mode, about 250 credits.
  • The audio-mode consent clip in public/voice/ was removed because its text changed. Warming the cache now costs ~1,349 credits, and GO_LIVE.md is updated (74 of 100 messages, ~3,400 credits).

The review

The co-worker was asked whether every fact the bot now states is true, and whether the work-type rule could hide a right she has. Round 1 requested changes. Round 2 approved.

  • blockingA mother fired while pregnant would be labelled "desempregada", and the rule then banned citing her stability. This is the bot's most important case. Fixed, and checked live.
  • blockingEvery "diarista" and every worker without carteira was treated as a non-employee, and "if unsure, don't cite" meant silence. Fixed, and checked live.
  • blockingA wrong work type had no way to be corrected. It's now confirmed with her, shown to her, and correctable.
  • fixed"Não guarda o nome" overstated, and the facts implied nobody else could read her messages. The vendor fact is added.
  • fixedRound 2 caught a regression from the greeting change: "Fui demitida grávida" is 3 words and would have been dropped. Greetings are now matched by list, with a test for that exact message.

Limits

  • ElevenLabs keeps the text of every voice reply in our account's history. The bot's facts only describe what we keep, so they stay true, but personal replies would sit there once VOICE_REPLIES=1. This should be closed before the voice test, by deleting each history item after it's generated.
  • The tests check the instructions, not Claude's behaviour. The live checks above were run once each. A different phrasing may still get a weaker answer, so read the demo answers.
  • laws.md still says stability "não vale para… desempregada". The new rule defines the term narrowly, but the law file wasn't changed, because that file belongs to the law-check work.
